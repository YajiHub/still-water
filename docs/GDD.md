# Still Water
Team: Jopur Jay Montecillo II (solo; commits as `YajiHub` and under my name) Platform: Android Orientation: landscape

> One-page Game Design Document in the format the lab requires (Phase 1, Step 1.2).
> A summary of the game as built (updated 2026-11-16); full details live in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## 1. Pitch
Memorize the safe path, jump across a flooded nightmare pool, and never make a splash, because something in the water is listening.

## 2. Genre and target players
3D horror platformer + memory challenge. Casual-to-mid-core mobile players aged roughly 13+ who enjoy short horror and skill-based platformers. Atmospheric scares, no gore.

## 3. Core mechanic and touch controls
Watch the safe platforms glow in sequence, then cross the water by **running and jumping** between them. Left thumb: floating virtual joystick (move). Right thumb: drag to look, Jump/Climb button (and PUSH online). First- or third-person camera, switchable in play and in Settings; a left-handed layout and a button-size slider in Settings. Fake platforms look the same but sink. Falling in causes a splash that wakes the Listener, and you must climb back out before it reaches you.

## 4. Goal, win condition, lose condition
- **Goal:** reach the Exit door on the far side of the pool.
- **Win:** touch the Exit door.
- **Lose:** caught by the Listener while in the water, or the level timer runs out.

## 5. Level plan
| Level | Goal | What is new or harder | Time limit (par for ★★★) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Shallow End | Remember the glowing path and reach the door | Basics: wide platforms, short gaps, a slow Listener | 120 s (60 s) |
| 2 Deep End | Cross without splashing for 3 stars | Less time, wider gaps, faster Listener | 110 s (55 s) |
| 3 Current | Time your jumps on drifting platforms | **New:** moving platforms (ferries), a bigger map | 180 s (95 s) |
| 4 Undertow | Find the way through | **New:** crumbling and one-way platforms; a Listener that reacts at once | 200 s (120 s) |
| 5 The Drain | Escape the Drain | Everything combined on the biggest map, fastest Listener | 220 s (140 s) |

## 6. Unlocking rule
Clearing level *n* unlocks level *n+1*. Progress is saved on the phone (`save.json`) before the result screen. Stars: ★ clear · ★★ ≤ 1 splash · ★★★ no splashes and under par time.

## 7. Offline mode
Solo campaign (levels 1–5), playable in airplane mode; the Listener is the computer-controlled opponent. No network code runs offline. (A "Ghost Swimmer" racing rival was planned but not built.)

## 8. Online mode
Local Multiplayer uses the same five rooms on two phones. One phone hosts on the hotspot and decides the result. The second joins by IP. Both can play with mobile data off.

Real-time **co-op race for 2–4 players**, on a Colyseus server (a laptop on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, or the free Render server on the internet). All five rooms are joined end to end with open doors: players race through them, score for each door and their finishing place, lose points for splashes and catches, and earn a team bonus if everyone gets through in time. Each room has its own Listener at its solo speed; anyone caught sees the catch, and restarts at that room's start. PUSH shoves another player. Data sent: positions (15×/s) and events (ready, push); the server sends 10 Hz snapshots plus catch/push events. The server validates moves, runs the Listeners, and **decides scores and places**. Online results do not affect offline unlocks. If the server is unreachable or the connection drops, a clear message appears on the Online page.

## 9. Screens
Title (also the mode select: Solo / Online), Level Select, Level (HUD), Pause (Resume, Retry, Sound On/Off, Menu), Result, Settings, Credits, Online lobby, Online Match, Online Result (leaderboard), connection-error message.

## 10. Art and sound style + asset sources
Liminal "poolrooms" horror: pale tiled halls, still green water, harsh light, fog. Low-to-mid poly. The Listener and the music are original (made in code with Blender's Python). Player bodies and animations: Quaternius (CC0). Pool tiles: ambientCG (CC0). Splash: Universfield on Pixabay. Player rig: Unity Starter Assets. All listed in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

## 11. Risks and simpler fallbacks
| Risk | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Jumping feels unfair on a phone | Bigger platforms, coyote time and edge forgiveness |
| Online real-time sync too hard | Server-authoritative positions at 10 Hz, with client prediction for the Listeners |
| Monster AI or animation too much work | Procedural rig and animations generated by script |
| Graphics too heavy for low-end phones | Plain animated water, fewer post-processing effects |
| Free internet server sleeping during the demo | The game waits up to 90 s for it to wake; or run the server on a laptop on the same Wi-Fi |
